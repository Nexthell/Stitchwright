Built for lettering

Most home embroidery software treats text as an afterthought. Stitchwright starts there: names on jackets, monograms on towels, initials on gifts.

Crisp satin lettering

Dense, even satin columns with the smooth, raised look of professionally embroidered text. Still being tuned, so test-stitch on scrap fabric first.

In preview

Tatami fill, one section at a time

Fills finish one part of a letter before moving on, instead of sweeping row by row across the gaps. Set the fill angle yourself, and stitch length adapts to the letter size.

In preview

Fewer jump stitches

Stitchwright routes the needle along the letters to get from one part to the next, the way a 3D printer plans its travel. That means less trimming afterwards.

In preview

Sewing-time estimate

See how long a design will take before you hoop it, based on your machine's stitch speed. Set to 400 stitches per minute for a Brother PP1, or change it to match yours.

In preview

Open-source fonts included

A set of free, openly licensed fonts comes bundled, ready to stitch. No font packs to buy before you start.

In preview

Works with most machines

Save as PES for Brother, DST for Tajima and most commercial machines, JEF for Janome, EXP for Melco and Bernina, VP3 for Husqvarna Viking and Pfaff, or XXX for Singer.

In preview

Industrial satin and more stitch types

Beyond plain satin: a factory-style industrial satin, shortest-span fill, contour fill and cross-hatch fill. Add a satin or running outline to any of them, with mitred corners on sharp angles.

In preview

SVG import with thread colours

Bring in your own vector logos as filled shapes, paint each area with a thread colour, and skip stitching where one shape sits on top of another. Image tracing from PNG may follow later.

In preview

Text to thread in four steps

  1. Type

    Write the name or initials and pick a font, or import an SVG.

  2. Tune

    Choose a stitch type, set the angle, size and outline, and check the preview.

  3. Save

    Save in your machine's format, with the sewing time and stitch count shown.

  4. Stitch

    Send it to your machine by USB stick or through your machine's app.
